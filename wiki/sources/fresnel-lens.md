The Fresnel lens, invented by [[augustin-fresnel]], replaced weak reflector systems. Its concentric rings focused light from a central lamp into a powerful horizontal beam visible for many miles. The design was adopted worldwide and dramatically improved maritime safety. See also [[fresnel-lens-optics]] and [[lighthouse-illumination]].
